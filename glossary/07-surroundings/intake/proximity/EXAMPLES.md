# Proximity examples

Proximity is how much of the source of what you take in reached you. Each row is one thing, taken in at each of the four levels, closest first.

| Kind | Example | Whole | Partial | Altered | Retold |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Media | Zizek's talk | In the room | The YouTube cut, or a livestream | A one-minute highlight clip | A friend telling you what he said |
| Media | A painting | The original | A print, or a photo of it | A meme made from it | Someone describing it |
| Media | A book | Reading it | An extract | A translation, or an audiobook | A review |
| Knowledge | A lecture | In the hall | The recording | Someone's slide summary | A classmate's notes |
| Communication | A conversation | Face to face | A phone call | A cropped screenshot of it | Someone telling you what was said |
| Ideas | A philosophy | Reading the thinker | An excerpt | A quote out of context | Someone's take on it |
| Spiritual | A sermon | In the room | The stream | A clip | Someone passing on the message |
| Commercial | A product | Trying it in the shop | A product video | The ad | A review |
| Observation | A sunset | Watching it | A photo of it | A filtered photo | Someone telling you it was beautiful |
| Experiences | A festival | Being there | The livestream | The aftermovie | A friend's story |
| Practical | A law | The law itself | One section of it | A plain-language version | Someone telling you what it says |
