# Overview

**Tier 3 forces are the interface between agents and reality** - they shape perception, drive behavior, and mediate transformation.

They are:
- **Agent-facing** - experienced subjectively by conscious entities
- **Derived from Tiers 1-2** - built from axioms and governors
- **Behavioral drivers** - create motion, choice, and change
- **Transformational** - can liberate or trap depending on relationship

Think of them as:
- The **user interface** (while Tiers 1-2 are backend logic)
- The **emotional/motivational** layer (while Tier 2 is regulatory)
- The **navigation system** (guides agents through possibility space)

**These forces are what it FEELS like to be alive and conscious.**
