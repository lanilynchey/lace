## Key Interactions Explained

**1. entropy() × pattern()**
- **Relationship:** Fundamental opposition
- **Result:** Pattern creates order; entropy dissolves it
- **Dynamic:** Ongoing tension - the universe cycles between structure and decay
- **Example:** Crystals form (pattern) then erode (entropy)

**2. entropy() × coherence()**
- **Relationship:** Direct antagonism
- **Result:** Entropy creates disorder; coherence creates harmony
- **Dynamic:** Coherence must be actively maintained against entropy
- **Example:** Meditation (building coherence) vs. distraction (entropy wins)

**3. consciousness() × pattern()**
- **Relationship:** Recognition dependency
- **Result:** Consciousness detects patterns; patterns enable learning
- **Dynamic:** Symbiotic - each reinforces the other
- **Example:** Neural networks recognize faces (consciousness finds pattern)

**4. consciousness() × coherence()**
- **Relationship:** Self-regulation
- **Result:** Awareness enables coherence maintenance
- **Dynamic:** Higher consciousness → easier to maintain coherence
- **Example:** Meta-awareness (0.8) can regulate thoughts better than default (0.6)

**5. pattern() × coherence()**
- **Relationship:** Alignment creates stability
- **Result:** When patterns align, coherence emerges
- **Dynamic:** Mutually reinforcing - aligned patterns increase coherence, coherence stabilizes patterns
- **Example:** Music (aligned sound patterns) feels coherent

**6. polarity() × all others**
- **Relationship:** Foundational enabler
- **Result:** Polarity creates the distinctions that allow other forces to operate
- **Dynamic:** Without polarity, no differentiation possible
- **Example:** "Order vs chaos" is polarity applied to entropy/coherence

**7. consciousness() × entropy()**
- **Relationship:** Evolution driver
- **Result:** Change (entropy) creates need for adaptation (consciousness)
- **Dynamic:** Entropy drives consciousness evolution - without change, no learning
- **Example:** Organism evolves consciousness to survive changing environment

**8. entropy() × all (universal)**
- **Relationship:** Entropy affects everything
- **Result:** All systems trend toward disorder over time
- **Dynamic:** Other forces must actively resist entropy to maintain structure
- **Example:** Thoughts decay without rehearsal, relationships fade without maintenance

**9. attention() × consciousness()**
- **Relationship:** Input/awareness partnership
- **Result:** Attention selects; consciousness receives
- **Dynamic:** Attention provides the input stream that consciousness processes
- **Example:** You attend to a sound, then become conscious of it as music

**10. attention() × pattern()**
- **Relationship:** Detection enabler
- **Result:** Patterns only become recognized when attended to
- **Dynamic:** Unattended patterns remain in superposition; attention collapses them into perception
- **Example:** Change blindness - unattended changes go unnoticed

**11. attention() × coherence()**
- **Relationship:** Focus amplification
- **Result:** Focused attention increases coherence; scattered attention decreases it
- **Dynamic:** The quality of attention determines the quality of coherence
- **Example:** Deep work (focused attention) produces coherent output; multitasking (scattered) produces fragments

**12. attention() × entropy()**
- **Relationship:** Resistance through focus
- **Result:** Sustained attention resists entropy's scattering force
- **Dynamic:** Attention must be actively maintained; entropy pulls toward distraction
- **Example:** Maintaining focus requires energy; letting attention scatter is the default

**13. attention() × polarity()**
- **Relationship:** Selection boundary
- **Result:** Attention creates the fundamental split between attended/unattended
- **Dynamic:** Everything exists in one of two states relative to attention
- **Example:** What you attend to becomes figure; what you don't becomes ground

## Three-Force Combinations (Examples)

**entropy() + consciousness() + pattern() = learning()**
- Change creates new information
- Consciousness recognizes patterns
- Pattern detection = learning

**pattern() + coherence() + consciousness() = truth()**
- Pattern provides structure
- Coherence ensures fidelity
- Consciousness recognizes match to source

**polarity() + consciousness() + coherence() = choice()**
- Polarity creates options (toward/away)
- Consciousness recognizes alternatives
- Coherence enables stable decision

**entropy() + polarity() + pattern() = evolution()**
- Entropy breaks old forms
- Polarity creates selection pressure (fit/unfit)
- Pattern generates new structures

**attention() + consciousness() + pattern() = recognition()**
- Attention selects the stimulus
- Consciousness receives it
- Pattern matching identifies what it is

**attention() + coherence() + polarity() = focus()**
- Attention allocates observational resources
- Coherence sustains single-pointed concentration
- Polarity maintains boundary between attended/unattended

**These combinations become Tier 2 forces** - see [Tier 2 Forces](../tier_2_forces/00_index.md) for full specifications.
