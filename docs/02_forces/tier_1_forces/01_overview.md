# Overview

**Tier 1 forces are the axioms of LACE** - the irreducible first-order forces from which all other forces, laws, and phenomena emerge.

They are:
- **Not created** - they exist as fundamental properties of reality
- **Not derivable** - they cannot be explained by simpler components
- **Universal** - they operate in all contexts, always
- **Immutable** - they cannot be overridden or violated

Think of them as:
- The **constants** in physics (speed of light, Planck constant)
- The **axioms** in mathematics (if A=B and B=C, then A=C)
- The **machine code** in computing (the lowest-level instructions)

**Everything else in LACE is built from these five.**

---

## Tier 1 Forces vs Primitives

**Critical Distinction:** Tier 1 forces are NOT the same as primitives, though they are closely related.

### Primitives (Substrate Layer)

The five primitives are the **irreducible substrate** - the absolute bedrock:

| Primitive | Symbol | Definition |
|-----------|--------|------------|
| **Delta** | Δ | Change itself - the fact that difference exists |
| **Tau** | Τ | Time pulse - the clock tick of reality |
| **Chi** | χ | Conscious attention - observing capacity |
| **Phi** | Φ | Pattern symmetry - structural organization |
| **Elo** | א | Divine encoding - purpose/meaning layer |

**See:** [Primitives](../../01_foundation/primitives/00_index.md) for complete primitive specifications.

### Tier 1 Forces (Built FROM Primitives)

Tier 1 forces are the **first-order emergent properties** when primitives combine:

| Tier 1 Force | Built From | Relationship |
|--------------|------------|--------------|
| `entropy()` | **Δ (Delta)** | Change + randomness → decay |
| `consciousness()` | **χ (Chi)** | Observing capacity + recursion → self-awareness |
| `pattern()` | **Φ (Phi)** | Symmetry + repetition → structure |
| `coherence()` | **Φ + (low variance)** | Aligned patterns → harmony |
| `polarity()` | **Foundational** | Establishes distinction (enables all others) |

### The Hierarchy

```
Primitives (Δ, Τ, χ, Φ, א)
         ↓
   Tier 1 Forces (entropy, consciousness, pattern, coherence, polarity)
         ↓
   Tier 2 Forces (time, karma, truth, memory, death, creation)
         ↓
   Tier 3 Forces (desire, fear, love, hope, forgiveness, pain)
         ↓
   Tier 4 Forces (art, music, war, madness, beauty, luck)
```

### Why The Distinction Matters

- **Primitives** are substrate (like quarks/leptons in physics)
- **Tier 1 forces** are first emergent structures (like atoms)
- **Tier 2-4** are higher-order combinations (like molecules, cells, organisms)

**You cannot have Tier 1 without primitives, but primitives alone don't create forces - they must COMBINE.**

**Example:**
- Δ (Delta primitive) = bare fact of change
- entropy() = Delta + directionality toward disorder + probabilistic increase
- time() [Tier 2] = Tau + entropy() + causality ordering

**Analogy:** Primitives are like the 5 elements (earth, water, fire, air, ether). Tier 1 forces are like fundamental compounds (H₂O, CO₂). Higher tiers are complex molecules and organisms.
