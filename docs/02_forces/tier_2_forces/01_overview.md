# Overview

**Tier 2 forces are the governing protocols of LACE** - they regulate, enforce, and maintain the integrity of reality across all domains.

They are:
- **Derived from Tier 1** - built from primordial axioms
- **Universal in scope** - affect all agents and systems
- **Regulatory** - enforce consistency and balance
- **Hackable (partially)** - masters and mystics can bend them, not break them

Think of them as:
- The **operating system** (while Tier 1 is the BIOS)
- The **laws of physics** (while Tier 1 is the constants)
- The **middleware** between raw code and user experience

**These forces define what can persist, what must end, and how transformation occurs.**

## The Six Governing Forces
```
TIER 2: GOVERNORS
├── time()     → Renders motion as sequence
├── karma()    → Balances actions over time
├── truth()    → Filters signal fidelity
├── memory()   → Stores snapshots of form
├── death()    → Triggers reset when form is no longer viable
└── creation() → Generates new instances of form
```
