# Why "Synthesis" Instead of "Inheritance"

Tier 4 forces use **"Synthesis:"** (not "Inheritance:") because they are composite phenomena - emergent combinations of forces from multiple tiers simultaneously - rather than direct descendants from single parents.

**Inheritance (Tiers 1-3):**
- Vertical dependency chain
- Child force inherits properties from parent forces
- Example: desire() inherits from polarity() + time() + memory()
- Linear descent: child ← parent ← grandparent

**Synthesis (Tier 4):**
- Horizontal combination
- Multiple peer forces coordinate to create emergent phenomenon
- Example: courage() synthesizes from fear() + hope() + will() + action
- No single "parent" - requires simultaneous activation of multiple forces
- Forces from different tiers combine as peers

**This reflects the nature of emergence:** Tier 4 forces don't exist independently - they only arise when specific combinations of lower forces activate together in context. Remove any component and the emergent force collapses.

**Analogy:**
- **Inheritance:** Like biological descent (you inherit DNA from parents)
- **Synthesis:** Like chemical compounds (H₂O emerges from H + O, but has properties neither has alone)
