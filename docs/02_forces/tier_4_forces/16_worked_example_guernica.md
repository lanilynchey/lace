# Worked Example: Guernica (1937)

### **Multi-Force Analysis of Picasso's Masterwork**

**Guernica** is one of the most powerful artworks of the 20th century - a large-scale painting depicting the bombing of the Basque town Guernica during the Spanish Civil War. It demonstrates multiple Tier 4 forces in synthesis.

### **Historical Context**
- **Date:** April 26, 1937
- **Event:** Nazi German and Fascist Italian warplanes bomb Guernica, killing civilians
- **Artist:** Pablo Picasso (Spanish expatriate in Paris)
- **Creation:** May-June 1937 (6 weeks of intense work)
- **Size:** 11 ft × 25.6 ft (massive mural-scale)
