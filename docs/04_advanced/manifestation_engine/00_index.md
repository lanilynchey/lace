## Key Concepts Summary

### The Core Equation
```
state_signature = belief + expectation + embodiment + subconscious_memory
```

Your state signature broadcasts a frequency. The field matches you to a worldline of similar vibration.

### The 7-Step Pipeline
1. **impulse()** - Desire arises
2. **encode_belief()** - Filters through beliefs
3. **emotion_charge()** - Adds emotional energy
4. **repetition_loop()** - Reinforces through repetition
5. **energetic_request()** - Broadcasts to field
6. **delay_buffer()** - Processes through law_delay
7. **deliver_outcome()** - Manifests in reality

### Why Manifestation Fails
1. **Trauma loop active** - Stuck in past pattern
2. **Signal incoherent** - Internal contradiction
3. **Unsafe to manifest** - System protection engaged

### Critical Insights
- **The body can't lie** - SOMA encodes truth, not words
- **Coherence is required** - Internal alignment is the cost
- **Creation AND matching** - Both/and, not either/or
- **Weighting matters** - Embodiment > Expectation > Belief > Memory
