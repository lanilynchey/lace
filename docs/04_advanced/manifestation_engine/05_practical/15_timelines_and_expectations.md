## Manifestation Timelines: What to Expect

**Typical timelines for different manifestation magnitudes:**

### **Small Shifts** (parking spot, coffee, small synchronicity)
- **Timeline:** Minutes to hours
- **Coherence Required:** 0.4-0.6 (low-moderate)
- **Examples:** Find parking, run into friend, get free coffee
- **Why Fast:** Low resistance, no safety blocks, small state mutation

### **Medium Shifts** (job, relationship, move)
- **Timeline:** Weeks to months
- **Coherence Required:** 0.6-0.8 (moderate-high)
- **Examples:** New job, relationship begins, apartment found
- **Why Medium:** Moderate complexity, some belief work needed, multiple synchronicities required

### **Large Shifts** (career change, major healing, significant wealth)
- **Timeline:** Months to years
- **Coherence Required:** 0.75-0.9 (high)
- **Examples:** Career pivot, heal chronic illness, 10x income increase
- **Why Slow:** Deep belief rewiring needed, safety blocks to clear, sustained coherence required

### **Massive Shifts** (life purpose, awakening, complete transformation)
- **Timeline:** Years to lifetime
- **Coherence Required:** 0.85-0.98 (very high to master)
- **Examples:** Find life purpose, spiritual awakening, become who you're meant to be
- **Why Lifetime:** Fundamental identity shift, ego dissolution required, entire trajectory redirection

**Factors Affecting Speed:**

**Accelerators (Faster Manifestation):**
- High coherence (all components aligned)
- Low karmic debt (few constraints)
- Deep embodiment (strong somatic match)
- Collective field support (others want this too)
- Soul contract alignment (it's your mission)

**Decelerators (Slower Manifestation):**
- Low coherence (internal conflict)
- Karmic blocks (debts to clear first)
- Safety blocks (nervous system resistance)
- Going against collective field
- Misaligned with soul contract (forcing wrong path)

**The Paradox:**
- Attachment to timeline SLOWS manifestation (desperation lowers coherence)
- Surrender of timeline SPEEDS manifestation (relaxation raises coherence)
- Best approach: Work on STATE, let TIMING handle itself
