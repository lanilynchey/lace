**Forces That Can Interfere:**
- **fear() [Tier 3]** - Can block or distort signal at encoding step (step 2) by triggering safety mechanisms
- **pain() [Tier 3]** - Alert signal indicating misalignment between desire and embodiment
- **hope() [Tier 3]** - Expands timeline aperture (aids manifestation by increasing possibility space)
- **suffering() [Tier 3]** - Indicates major disconnect (triggers crash condition #1)
- **forgiveness() [Tier 3]** - Can clear karmic blocks in delay_buffer (step 6)

**Forces That Amplify:**
- **gratitude() [Tier 3]** - Increases coherence, strengthens signal quality
- **love() [Tier 3]** - Creates resonance, attracts matching frequencies
- **presence() [Tier 3]** - Reduces subconscious noise, improves embodiment match

**Integration Note:** Every manifestation is a complex interplay of multiple forces. The pipeline shows primary forces at each step, but all forces influence the process systemically. See [Interaction Mechanics](../../../03_mechanics/interaction_mechanics/00_index.md) for force composition rules.
