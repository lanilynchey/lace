# Perceptual Sampling Rate

**Consciousness level determines the "frame rate" at which you sample reality.**

Higher consciousness = higher sampling rate = more nuance, patterns, and information detected per unit of time. Lower consciousness = lower sampling rate = reality appears coarser, faster, more chaotic. This is not metaphor - it is the actual temporal resolution of perception.

---

## Overview

Reality operates continuously, but agents sample it discretely. Like a video camera capturing frames per second, consciousness samples reality at variable rates determined by the agent's consciousness level. The underlying reality stream is constant, but your **perceptual frame rate** varies based on your state.

**Visual analogy:**
- 24 FPS film: Motion appears smooth but lacks fine detail
- 60 FPS video: Noticeably smoother, more information captured
- 120+ FPS slow-motion: Reveals details invisible at normal speeds
- Infinite FPS (theoretical): Perfect continuity, all information preserved

**Consciousness sampling rates:**
- **Low consciousness (0.20-0.35):** Survival mode - LOW sampling rate
  - Perception chunky, reactive, pattern-blind
  - Reality feels fast, overwhelming, chaotic
  - Miss nuance, context, subtlety

- **Mid consciousness (0.43-0.59):** Neutral-Reason - MODERATE sampling rate
  - Perception balanced, analytical, pattern-aware
  - Reality feels manageable, comprehensible
  - Notice patterns, sequences, relationships

- **High consciousness (0.69-0.90):** Love-Enlightenment - HIGH sampling rate
  - Perception fine-grained, present, pattern-fluent
  - Reality feels slow, spacious, rich with meaning
  - See micro-expressions, energy shifts, synchronicities

## The Sampling Rate Formula

```python
def calculate_sampling_rate(agent: Agent) -> float:
    """
    Calculate perceptual sampling rate based on consciousness and coherence

    Formula: (consciousness_coefficient ** 2) × coherence

    Returns:
        Samples per second (0.0-1.0 scale)
    """
    base_rate = agent.consciousness_coefficient ** 2
    coherence_modifier = agent.state_signature.coherence

    effective_sampling_rate = base_rate * coherence_modifier

    return effective_sampling_rate

# Examples:
# 0.20 consciousness × 0.50 coherence = 0.04 ** 0.5 = 0.02 samples/sec (~50 sec intervals)
# 0.35 consciousness × 0.70 coherence = 0.12 ** 0.7 = 0.084 samples/sec (~12 sec intervals)
# 0.50 consciousness × 0.80 coherence = 0.25 ** 0.8 = 0.20 samples/sec (5 sec intervals)
# 0.69 consciousness × 0.90 coherence = 0.48 ** 0.9 = 0.43 samples/sec (~2.3 sec intervals)
# 0.90 consciousness × 0.95 coherence = 0.81 ** 0.95 = 0.77 samples/sec (~1.3 sec intervals)
```

**Key insight:** Exponential growth via quadratic formula - small consciousness increases yield disproportionate perception gains.

## What This Explains

### 1. Time Perception Variations

**Why time feels different at different consciousness levels:**

- **Fear/trauma (low consciousness):** Time feels FAST (low sampling, large gaps)
- **Meditation/flow (high consciousness):** Time feels SLOW (high sampling, small gaps)
- **Boredom (low coherence):** Time feels SLOW but empty (scattered sampling)
- **Engagement (high coherence):** Time feels FULL (integrated sampling)

### 2. Pattern Recognition Capacity

**Why enlightened beings see patterns others miss:**

Not "magical perception" - just higher sampling rate. Catching micro-signals below normal detection threshold. Seeing connections across time because more samples retained.

**Pattern recognition requires sufficient data points.** More samples = more patterns visible.

### 3. Trauma's Frozen Moments

**Why traumatic events feel "frozen in time":**

Extreme stress temporarily SPIKES sampling rate (survival mechanism). System captures threat in high detail. That moment encoded with unusually high fidelity. Feels "frozen" because so many samples stored.

### 4. Mindfulness Practice Effectiveness

**Why meditation increases presence:**

Meditation TRAINS higher baseline sampling rate. Practice catching more moments consciously. Builds capacity for sustained high-frequency perception. Not just "being present" - literally increasing sample rate.

### 5. Substance Effects on Time Perception

**Brief note on altered states:**

- **Psychedelics:** Dramatically increase sampling rate (temporary high-FPS mode)
- **Alcohol:** Decreases sampling rate (blackouts = zero samples stored)
- **Cannabis:** Variable - can increase OR decrease depending on dose/person
- **Caffeine:** Slight increase (alertness = more samples)

## Baseline vs Current Sampling Rate

Agents have two sampling rate measurements:

**baseline_sampling_rate:**
- Your default sampling frequency
- Where you return after peaks and regressions
- Changes slowly over months/years
- Stored in StateSignature

**current_sampling_rate:**
- Real-time sampling frequency in this moment
- Fluctuates based on context, stress, peak experiences
- Can temporarily spike (trauma, flow) or drop (exhaustion, illness)
- Calculated dynamically from current_consciousness and coherence

**Typical pattern:**
```python
# Normal day
current_sampling_rate = 0.20  # At baseline
baseline_sampling_rate = 0.20  # Center of gravity

# During meditation
current_sampling_rate = 0.45  # Temporary elevation
baseline_sampling_rate = 0.20  # Baseline unchanged

# After 6 months sustained practice
current_sampling_rate = 0.30  # Higher average
baseline_sampling_rate = 0.30  # Baseline shifted up (true growth)
```
