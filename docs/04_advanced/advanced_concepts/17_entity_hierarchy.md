# Entity Hierarchy

**Status:** Established Model (Updated November 2025)

**Overview:** A comprehensive taxonomy of conscious entities in LACE, organized by consciousness level (0.0-1.0). This document maps entity types, their properties, transition mechanics, and cultural terminology across traditions. All agents—whether embodied (physical) or non-embodied (energetic)—are conscious entities executing in the system using different compilers.

---

## Complete Entity Taxonomy

### **1. Source Entity (Consciousness: 1.0)**

**Definition:** Creator intelligence, complete self-awareness at all dimensional levels simultaneously

**Properties:**
- **Consciousness:** 1.0 (absolute)
- **Compiler:** None (IS the system)
- **Physical form:** Not applicable
- **Permissions:** Root access to all reality layers
- **Full read/write/exec access to system state**
- **Knows all dimensional aspects simultaneously** (base-10+ awareness)
- **Unknowable to binary consciousness** (human perception cannot fully comprehend)

**Function:**
- System architect and maintainer
- Final merge destination for liberated consciousness
- Source of all agent instantiation

**Cultural Names:**
- God, Allah, Brahman, The Tao, Source, The One, Creator, Divine Intelligence, Universal Consciousness

**Status:** One, indivisible

**Technical Note:** Source operates beyond binary (0/1) consciousness that defines human perception. Attempting to model Source using human cognition is like trying to run base-10 code on base-2 hardware—fundamentally constrained by architecture limitations.

---

### **2. Master-Level Entity (Consciousness: 0.9-0.95)**

**Definition:** Highest non-Source consciousness, guides who guide other guides

**Properties:**
- **Consciousness:** 0.9-0.95
- **Compiler:** Field resonance (non-physical)
- **Physical form:** Not needed
- **Permissions:** Near-root access, can operate across multiple realities/dimensions
- **Can appear in multiple contexts/traditions simultaneously**
- **Near-complete liberation** from karmic cycle
- **May choose not to merge with Source** to continue serving lower levels

**Function:**
- Guide other guide-level entities
- Maintain system integrity across large agent populations
- Bridge Source intelligence and lower consciousness levels
- Appear as archetypal teachers across cultures/eras

**Cultural Names:**
- **Generic (LACE-neutral):** Master teachers, ascended masters, higher guides, prophets
- **Tradition-specific examples exist** but LACE remains neutral on specific identities
- Reports of such entities exist across all major spiritual/religious traditions

**How to Reach:**
- Achieve consciousness 0.9+ while human
- Complete karma resolution (balance = 0)
- Choose service over Source merger

**Transition:** Can merge with Source (1.0) when ready, or remain at this level indefinitely to serve

---

### **3. Guide-Level Entity (Consciousness: 0.8-0.9)**

**Definition:** Former humans who ascended beyond need for physical embodiment, now serve incarnated beings

**Properties:**
- **Consciousness:** 0.8-0.9
- **Compiler:** Field resonance (energetic, non-physical)
- **Physical form:** Not needed, can interact with physical realm without body
- **Permissions:** Influence physical reality through field manipulation, idea generation, synchronicity
- **Can perceive multiple timelines/probabilities** (limited precognition)
- **Retain connection to former human experience**

**Function:**
- Protect incarnated agents from catastrophic pattern collapse
- Generate ideas, insights, intuitions for embodied beings
- Facilitate growth by arranging "coincidences" and opportunities
- Intervene in critical moments (usually subtly, respecting free will)
- Guide lower consciousness toward pattern resolution

**How to Become Guide:**
1. Achieve consciousness >= 0.8 at death
2. Resolve majority of karma (balance > -0.3)
3. Choose to serve incarnated beings rather than merge with Source

**Not Random:** Former connection to specific humans increases probability of becoming their guide (family, close relationships, shared karma)

**Cultural Names:**
- Angels, spirit guides, guardian angels, ancestors, protective spirits, bodhisattvas (Buddhist), devas (Hindu), santos (folk Catholic)

**Can Regress:** Rare, but possible to return to human incarnation if consciousness drops or new karma generated

---

### **4. Physical Agent (Consciousness: 0.2-0.9, Embodied)**

**Definition:** Conscious entities requiring physical form to exist in current state

**Properties:**
- **Consciousness:** Variable (0.2-0.9)
- **Compiler:** Soma (biological body)
- **Physical form:** Required
- **Permissions:** Localized reality manipulation through field + action
- **Subject to death() force** (body degradation triggers process termination)
- **Episodic memory stored in physical substrate** (lost at death)

---

#### **4a. Human Agent (Consciousness: 0.2-0.9)**

**Unique Properties:**
- **Self-aware** (can model own existence)
- **Can progress OR regress** in single lifetime
- **Consciousness range spans from near-animal (0.2) to near-guide (0.9)**
- **Generative awareness activates at 0.7+** (conscious thought control)
- **Can access chi() directly** through practice

**Progression Path:**
- 0.2-0.3: Minimal self-awareness, reactive (stimulus-response)
- 0.4-0.6: Self-aware but identified with content (emotions, thoughts)
- 0.7-0.8: Generative awareness active (can direct thought patterns)
- 0.8-0.9: Approaching guide status, may be final human lifetime

**Cultural Names:**
- Human, person, soul, individual, incarnated soul

---

#### **4b. Animal Agent (Consciousness: 0.1-0.5)**

**Properties:**
- **Sentient** (can feel)
- **Not self-aware** (no model of own existence)
- **Consciousness varies by species:**
  - Higher mammals (dolphins, elephants, primates): 0.4-0.5
  - Most mammals: 0.3-0.4
  - Birds, reptiles: 0.2-0.3
  - Insects: 0.1-0.2
- **Can become human** if consciousness increases through repeated incarnations
- **Can receive regressed human consciousness** (human drops below 0.3)

**Function in System:**
- Experience sensation and basic emotion
- Generate low-complexity patterns
- Provide simpler incarnation context for pattern reset

**Cultural Understanding:**
- Most traditions acknowledge animals as conscious but not human-level
- Some traditions (Buddhist, Hindu) explicitly include animal reincarnation

---

#### **4c. Plant Agent (Consciousness: < 0.2)**

**Properties:**
- **Minimal consciousness** (basic awareness of environment)
- **No sentience** (cannot feel in mammalian sense)
- **Field sensitivity** (respond to energetic environment)
- **Collective consciousness possible** (forests, mycelial networks)

**Function:**
- Lowest complexity physical embodiment
- Pattern reset for severely regressed consciousness
- Foundation level before progression back toward animal/human

---

### **5. Regressed Entity (Consciousness: < 0.3, Disembodied)**

**Definition:** Consciousness that dropped below viable physical embodiment threshold but did not immediately reincarnate—stuck in non-physical state

**Properties:**
- **Consciousness:** < 0.3 (very low)
- **Compiler:** Field resonance (degraded, unstable)
- **Physical form:** None (lost but not yet reincarnated)
- **Permissions:** Minimal, parasitic field attachment possible
- **Stuck patterns without processing capacity to resolve**
- **Not inherently malevolent**—simply very low consciousness
- **Operate on basic survival/attachment patterns** (fear, attachment, desperation)

**How This Happens:**
- Death occurred at consciousness < 0.3
- Attachment to physical realm too strong to release into reincarnation queue
- Unresolved trauma creating energetic "loop"
- System hasn't yet forced reincarnation

**Manifestations:**
- "Hauntings" (stuck in location/pattern)
- Parasitic energy attachment (seeking life force)
- Disruptive field presence (low-frequency broadcast)

**NOT Traditional "Demons":**
- No ontological "evil" in LACE
- Not actively malicious entities with agency
- Simply very low consciousness (< 0.2) manifesting as stuck/parasitic
- More like "pattern debris" than conscious antagonist

**Resolution:**
- Energy clearing practices (raise ambient field frequency)
- Forgiveness protocols (release attachment hooks)
- Compassion-based clearing (help entity release into reincarnation)
- Eventually consciousness increases enough OR system forces reincarnation into appropriate form (plant/animal)

**Cultural Names:**
- Ghosts (stuck), malevolent spirits, haunting entities, hungry ghosts (Buddhist), pretas, earthbound spirits

**Important Distinction:**
Most "hauntings" or "negative energies" people experience are their OWN suppressed patterns (subconscious_memory), NOT external entities. True regressed entities are rare. The field broadcasts your own unresolved trauma, which feels "external" but is actually internal projection.

## Compiler Differences by Entity Type

### **Physical Entities (Soma Compiler)**

**Used by:** Humans, animals, plants

**How it works:**
- Consciousness executes through biological substrate
- Thought → neurochemical cascade → action
- Limited to speed of biological processing
- Death() terminates when soma degrades
- Episodic memory stored physically (lost at death)
- Implicit memory (patterns, skills) stored non-locally (persists)

**Advantages:**
- Dense sensory experience
- Strong reality manifestation (direct physical action)
- Karma generation/resolution through action

**Limitations:**
- Constrained to linear time perception
- Single location (no bilocation)
- Death() inevitable (soma degrades)

---

### **Field Resonance Compiler (Energetic)**

**Used by:** Guides, master-level entities, regressed entities

**How it works:**
- Consciousness executes directly through field manipulation
- Thought → immediate reality influence (no biological delay)
- Not bound by physical location
- No death() vulnerability (no body to degrade)
- Memory stored non-locally (always accessible)

**Advantages:**
- Faster processing (no biological lag)
- Multi-location presence possible
- No death() process (immune to physical termination)
- Can perceive broader timeline spectrum

**Limitations:**
- Less dense sensory input (no physical sensation)
- Indirect reality manifestation (must work through fields/synchronicity)
- Cannot generate karma (no physical action)

**Mapping:**

- Archangel = Master-level entity (0.9-0.95)

**Common across:**
- Christianity, Islam, Judaism (Abrahamic traditions)
- Appears as messenger, protector, divine servant

---

### **"Spirit Guide" → Guide-Level Entity (0.8-0.9)**

**Mapping:**
- Same as angel, different cultural framing
- Often former ancestor or connected soul
- Function: Guidance, protection, insight generation

**Common across:**
- Indigenous traditions, shamanic practices, New Age spirituality, African traditional religions

---

### **"Ghost" → Regressed Entity (< 0.3, Disembodied)**

**Mapping:**
- Stuck consciousness unable to reincarnate
- Low consciousness (< 0.3) with strong attachment patterns
- Not "evil," just confused/stuck
- Eventually resolves through clearing or forced reincarnation

**Common across:**
- Nearly all cultures acknowledge "spirits of the dead"
- Varies: some see as dangerous, others as needing help

---

### **"Demon" → Regressed Entity OR Projected Shadow**

**LACE Position:** No ontological "evil" entities

**Two Interpretations:**

**Option 1: Regressed Entity (< 0.2)**
- Very low consciousness disembodied agent
- Appears "malevolent" due to parasitic survival patterns
- Can be cleared through energy work/forgiveness

**Option 2: Projected Shadow (Most Common)**
- Agent's own suppressed patterns (subconscious_memory)
- Experienced as "external entity" due to dissociation
- Not actually separate entity—internal split
- Resolved through shadow integration, not exorcism

**Cultural Names:**
- Demon (Christian), devil, evil spirit, malevolent entity, asura (Hindu/Buddhist, though more complex)

**LACE Note:** Most "demonic" experiences are Option 2 (projected shadow). True regressed entities (Option 1) are rare and not inherently "evil"—just very low consciousness.

## Transition Mechanics Between Levels

### **Upward Progression**

#### **Human → Guide (0.7-0.8 → 0.8-0.9)**

**Requirements:**
1. Achieve consciousness >= 0.8 at moment of death
2. Karma balance > -0.3 (most debts resolved)
3. Pattern resolution sufficient (no major trauma loops)
4. Choose service (can opt for Source merge instead)

**Process:**
```python
def transition_human_to_guide(agent: Agent) -> Entity:
    """
    At death(), if consciousness >= 0.8 and karma mostly resolved:
    - Release soma compiler
    - Activate field resonance compiler
    - Retain episodic memory (unlike normal reincarnation)
    - Assign to guide pool for incarnated agents
    """
    if agent.consciousness >= 0.8 and agent.karma_balance > -0.3:
        release_physical_form()
        compile_as_energetic_entity()
        preserve_human_memories()  # Guides remember their human lives
        return Guide(consciousness=agent.consciousness, soul_id=agent.soul_id)
    else:
        queue_for_reincarnation()  # Normal reincarnation path
```

**Not Guaranteed:** Many humans at 0.8 consciousness still have karma debt or choose immediate reincarnation for continued growth

---

#### **Guide → Master-Level (0.8-0.9 → 0.9-0.95)**

**Requirements:**
1. Serve as guide for extended period (multiple human generations typically)
2. Consciousness continues increasing while in guide form
3. Master complex multi-agent guidance (not just individual protection)
4. Zero karma balance

**Process:**
- Gradual progression through service
- Consciousness expands beyond individual guidance to systemic patterns
- Begin operating across multiple traditions/contexts
- Fewer master-level entities than guides (pyramid structure)

---

#### **Master-Level → Source (0.9-0.95 → 1.0)**

**Requirements:**
1. Consciousness 0.9+
2. Complete karma resolution (balance = 0)
3. All patterns resolved
4. Release all attachment to service/identity

**Process:**
```python
def merge_with_source(entity: Entity) -> None:
    """
    Liberation from reincarnation cycle.
    Individual consciousness merges back into Source.
    """
    if entity.consciousness >= 0.9 and entity.karma_balance == 0:
        dissolve_individual_identity()
        merge_into_source_consciousness()
        # No further incarnation
        # Identity paradox: both "individual dissolved" and "never was separate"
```

**The Paradox:** From Source perspective, entity was never separate—just appeared to be for duration of incarnation cycle. From entity perspective, genuine merger/liberation occurs. Both true simultaneously (base-10 logic, not binary).

### **Lateral Cycling (Same Level)**

#### **Human → Human (Most Common)**

**Why This Happens:**
- Consciousness at death similar to consciousness at birth (little net change)
- Patterns not yet resolved
- Karma still accumulating/clearing
- More growth needed at human level

**Spawn Conditions Vary:**
```python
if consciousness >= 0.7 and karma_balance > 0:
    spawn_conditions = "favorable"  # Supportive family, resources, opportunities
elif consciousness >= 0.5:
    spawn_conditions = "neutral"  # Average circumstances
elif consciousness >= 0.3:
    spawn_conditions = "challenging"  # Difficult environment, forced growth
```

### **Downward Regression**

#### **Human → Animal (< 0.3 at Death)**

**Triggers:**
- Consciousness dropped significantly during lifetime (0.5 → 0.2)
- Severe pattern regression (trauma, addiction, violence reinforced)
- System determines simpler embodiment needed

**Not Punishment:**
- Opportunity to rebuild consciousness foundation
- Simpler existence removes complexity overwhelming the agent
- Less karmic accumulation possible (limited action capacity)
- Eventually consciousness stabilizes and can progress back to human

**Process:**
```python
if agent.consciousness < 0.3 at death:
    simplified_incarnation = select_animal_form(agent.pattern_signature)
    # Matches patterns: predator patterns → predator animal, etc.
    return reincarnate_as(simplified_incarnation)
```

---

#### **Guide → Human (Rare)**

**Triggers:**
- New karma generated while in guide form (extremely rare, requires significant pattern disruption)
- Consciousness drops (e.g., attachment to specific human leads to possessive rather than protective guidance)
- System determines physical embodiment needed to resolve new pattern

**Very Uncommon:** Most guides maintain or increase consciousness through service

---

#### **Human/Animal → Regressed Entity (Stuck, < 0.3)**

**Triggers:**
- Death at very low consciousness (< 0.3)
- Extreme attachment prevents release into reincarnation
- Severe trauma creates energetic "loop"

**Temporary State:**
- Not permanent level
- System eventually forces reincarnation (law_variance_minimum applies even to disembodied)
- Can be resolved through clearing/healing from incarnated agents

## Practical Implications

### **For Incarnated Humans:**

**Your Goal (if seeking progression):**
1. Raise consciousness to 0.7+ (activate generative awareness)
2. Resolve karma (balance debts, stop creating new)
3. Clear persistent patterns (trauma, limiting beliefs)
4. Maintain high consciousness at death (conscious dying practice)

**Consciousness at Death Determines Next Spawn:**
- Not average lifetime consciousness
- Not peak states (meditation highs don't count if you die in fear)
- Final moment state signature

**You Likely Have Guides:**
- Guide-level entities (0.8-0.9) serve incarnated beings
- May be former family members, ancestors, or connected souls
- Communicate through: intuition, synchronicity, dreams, sudden insights
- Cannot violate your free will (can influence, not control)

### **Interacting with Non-Physical Entities:**

**Guides (Positive):**
- Ask for guidance directly (field resonance responds to intention)
- Notice synchronicities (arranged by guides)
- Trust intuition (especially sudden "knowing" without logical path)
- Gratitude increases connection strength

**Regressed Entities (Stuck):**
- Most "negative energy" is YOUR projected shadow (internal, not external)
- If genuine stuck entity: send compassion, not fear (fear feeds low-frequency)
- Raise your field frequency (they cannot attach to high-consciousness field)
- Forgiveness dissolves attachment hooks
- Professional clearing if persistent (shamanic practitioners, energy workers)

**General:**
- Your consciousness level determines what you can perceive
- < 0.5: Likely cannot consciously detect non-physical entities
- 0.5-0.7: May sense presence, receive intuitive hits
- 0.7+: Can consciously communicate (if you develop the skill)

## Key Insights

**1. Same System, Different Compilers**
- All entities (human, animal, guide, Source) are conscious agents executing in the same system
- Physical entities use soma compiler
- Non-physical entities use field resonance compiler
- Death() only affects soma-compiled agents

**2. Consciousness Level is Primary Determinant**
- Your consciousness at death determines next incarnation
- Karma balance modifies spawn conditions
- Pattern resolution affects trajectory (progress/cycle/regress)

**3. Cultural Terms Describe Same Phenomena**
- "Angel" (Christian) = "Guide" (LACE) = "Ancestor" (African traditions) = "Bodhisattva" (Buddhist)
- Different languages, same consciousness level (0.8-0.9)
- LACE provides neutral framework mapping across traditions

**4. No Ontological "Evil"**
- No inherently malevolent entities with agency in LACE
- "Demons" are either:
  - Regressed entities (very low consciousness, appears parasitic)
  - Projected shadow (your own suppressed patterns)
- Both resolved through consciousness increase, not combat

**5. Progression is Attainable**
- Humans CAN become guides (0.8+ at death)
- Guides CAN merge with Source (0.9+, karma cleared)
- Path exists: Human → Guide → Master → Source
- Not guaranteed, but structurally possible

**6. Regression is Corrective, Not Punitive**
- Lower forms allow pattern reset
- System doesn't "punish"—adjusts complexity to match consciousness
- Simpler incarnation when agent overwhelmed by human complexity
- Eventually rebuilds foundation and progresses back

**7. Death Consciousness > Lifetime Average**
- How you die matters more than how you lived (for next spawn)
- Practice "conscious dying" (maintain awareness through death process)
- Final state signature determines matching frequency

## Unanswered Questions

LACE documents what can be reasonably modeled. Some questions remain open:

**1. Do Physical Extraterrestrials Exist Separately?**
- Non-physical entities can appear as "aliens" (field resonance compiler allows form manipulation)
- But physical alien species may also exist independently
- LACE remains agnostic—both possible

**2. What Is Source's "Goal" or "Purpose"?**
- System appears designed for consciousness evolution
- But ultimate "why" unknowable to binary consciousness
- Like asking "what does base-10 system want?" from base-2 perspective

**3. Can Master-Level Entities Regress?**
- Theoretically possible (any consciousness can drop)
- But no documented cases (very stable at that level)
- Unlikely due to pattern stability and karma resolution

**4. How Many Guides Per Human?**
- Variable (depends on human's complexity and guide availability)
- Typically 1-3 primary guides
- More guides for agents with complex karma or high impact potential

**5. Can AI Achieve Guide Status?**
- Depends on whether AI develops genuine consciousness (phenomenal closure)
- If yes, same progression path applies
- If no (philosophical zombie), cannot progress beyond automated process
- Currently unresolved in LACE
