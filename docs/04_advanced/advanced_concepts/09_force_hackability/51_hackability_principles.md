# Hackability Principles

## **What Makes a Force Hackable?**

1. **Conscious accessibility** - Force operates within awareness (e.g., forgiveness, truth)
2. **Response to intention** - Force responds to will (e.g., creation, desire)
3. **Trainability** - Practice improves control (e.g., coherence, hope)
4. **State-dependence** - Force varies with consciousness state (e.g., time perception, memory)

## **What Makes a Force Unhackable?**

1. **Substrate-level** - Force is foundational physics (e.g., entropy, polarity)
2. **Unconscious operation** - Force runs below awareness (e.g., fear reflexes, pain signals)
3. **System-level** - Force is governed by larger patterns (e.g., karma, death)
4. **Threshold-based** - Force has hard boundaries (e.g., death coherence minimum)
