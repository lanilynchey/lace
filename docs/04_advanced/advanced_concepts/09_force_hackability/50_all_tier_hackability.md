# All-Tier Hackability Reference

## **Tier 1 Forces (Primordial Axioms)**

| Force | Hackability | Why |
|-------|-------------|-----|
| **entropy()** | Very Low | Fundamental physical law; can't reverse entropy locally without increasing it elsewhere |
| **consciousness()** | Medium | Can expand awareness through practices, but baseline level difficult to shift permanently |
| **pattern()** | Medium | Can recognize and manipulate patterns, but underlying structure rigid |
| **coherence()** | High | Achievable through alignment practices, meditation, truth-telling |
| **polarity()** | Very Low | Binary consciousness is hardwired; can't escape polarity without transcending base-2 |

## **Tier 3 Forces (Interface/Emotional)**

| Force | Hackability | Why |
|-------|-------------|-----|
| **desire()** | High | Can redirect desires through awareness, sublimation, conscious choice |
| **fear()** | Medium | Can manage fear responses but survival wiring runs deep |
| **love()** | Medium | Can cultivate love but can't force genuine connection |
| **hope()** | High | Highly trainable through optimism practices, future visioning |
| **forgiveness()** | Very High | Entirely within conscious control, though emotionally difficult |
| **pain()** | Low | Pain signals automatic; can modulate response but not eliminate signal |
| **judgment()** | High | Can retrain judgment patterns through awareness and practice |

## **Tier 4 Forces (Emergent Complexity)**

| Force | Hackability | Why |
|-------|-------------|-----|
| **purpose()** | High | Can define and redefine purpose consciously |
| **connection()** | Medium | Can seek connection but can't force resonance |
| **freedom()** | Very High | Liberation through awareness, can choose freedom at any moment |
| **beauty()** | High | Beauty perception trainable through aesthetic cultivation |
| **suffering()** | Low | Suffering arises automatically from resistance; hard to prevent |
| **madness()** | Very Low | System corruption difficult to control once initiated |
| **wisdom()** | Medium | Cultivatable through experience but can't rush authentic wisdom |
| **transcendence()** | Low | Rare state; achievable but not controllable |
