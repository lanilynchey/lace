# Tier 2 Mastery: Manipulation & Influence

**Goal:** Work with (not override) governing forces

**Duration:** Years to decades of dedicated practice

**Prerequisites:**
- Coherence >= 0.7 sustained
- Tier 1 mastery established
- Consistent meditation/inner work practice

**Practices:**

**1. Time Manipulation (working with Τ)**
- **Flow state cultivation** - High coherence slows Tau subjectively
- **Presence practice** - Expand "now" awareness (5-10 min daily)
- **Trajectory redirection** - Consciously choose responses that redirect where the state mutates next
- **Result:** Subjective time control, ability to "slow down" critical moments

**2. Karma Transmutation**
- **Forgiveness work** - Neutralize karmic charge on past actions
- **Proactive balancing** - "Pay forward" before debt accumulates
- **Pattern completion** - Resolve loops before they recurse
- **Result:** Lighter karmic load, fewer "fated" encounters

**3. Truth Alignment**
- **Radical honesty** - Reduce dissonance between internal/external
- **Authentic expression** - Say what you mean
- **Illusion detection** - See through narratives and constructs
- **Result:** Reality responds more directly to intentions

**4. Memory Integration (working with Τ + χ)**
- **Trauma healing** - Reduce charge on suppressed memories
- **Reframe past** - Update interpretation of events
- **Akashic access** - High coherence (0.85+) queries past-life data
- **Result:** Subconscious distortion decreases, manifestation improves

**5. Death Mastery**
- **Ego death practice** - Temporary dissolution (meditation, breathwork)
- **Surrender practice** - Release control, trust process
- **Rebirth cycles** - Consciously "die" to old patterns
- **Result:** Fear of death decreases, transformation capacity increases

**6. Creation Activation**
- **Manifestation practice** - Align state_signature coherently
- **Co-creation** - Work WITH field, not against
- **Generative awareness** - Edit your own code consciously
- **Result:** Rapid materialization, synchronicities increase dramatically

**Milestone:** You can influence (not override) Tier 2 forces. Time feels malleable. Manifestation becomes consistent.

**Coherence Threshold:** 0.7-0.9 (high sustained coherence)
