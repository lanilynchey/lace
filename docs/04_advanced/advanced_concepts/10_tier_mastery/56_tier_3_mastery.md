# Tier 3 Mastery: Inner Alchemy

**Goal:** Transform interface forces (emotions/drives)

**Duration:** Ongoing (these forces never "complete")

**Prerequisites:**
- Some Tier 2 mastery (karma work, memory integration)
- Coherence >= 0.6 baseline
- Willingness to face shadow

**Practices:**

**1. Desire Purification**
- **Distinguish authentic desire from conditioned wanting**
- **Transmute craving into creative drive**
- **Align desires with deeper purpose (א primitive)**
- **Result:** Desires become fuel, not distraction

**2. Fear Alchemy**
- **Face fears directly** - Exposure, breathwork, somatic release
- **Transmute fear → courage** (Tier 4 synthesis)
- **Recognize fear as simulation, not reality**
- **Result:** Fear becomes information, not paralysis

**3. Love Cultivation**
- **Expand resonance bandwidth** - Love more beings/things
- **Release attachment** - Love without clinging
- **Practice unconditional regard**
- **Result:** Love becomes stable baseline, not conditional reaction

**4. Hope Grounding**
- **Balance optimism with realism**
- **Hope as aperture** (opening future), not delusion
- **Cultivate agency alongside hope**
- **Result:** Hope empowers action, doesn't replace it

**5. Forgiveness Mastery**
- **Release resentment** - Discharge karmic hooks (field hygiene)
- **Forgive self** - Reduce shame/guilt loops
- **Understand forgiveness** - Field hygiene, not condoning
- **Result:** Coherence increases, past stops contaminating present

**6. Pain Integration**
- **Presence with suffering** - Not avoidance
- **Extract wisdom from pain**
- **Use suffering as evolutionary pressure**
- **Result:** Pain becomes teacher, not enemy

**7. Suffering Liberation**
- **Recognize suffering = misalignment signal**
- **Adjust course when pain arises**
- **Break identification with pain body**
- **Result:** Suffering decreases as alignment improves

**Milestone:** Emotional mastery. You feel forces but aren't controlled by them.

**Coherence Threshold:** 0.6-0.8 (fluctuates with inner work)
