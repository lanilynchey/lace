- Context-dependent pattern matching

---

*"Death is the system's memory write."*

- Not deletion - data finalization
- Uploads experience to Akashic
- Form ends, information persists

---

**creation():**
*"Creation is the native act of divine agents."*

- Not forcing - allowing crystallization
- Requires fertile field state (presence + focus + permission)
- Obeys structure but allows emergence
