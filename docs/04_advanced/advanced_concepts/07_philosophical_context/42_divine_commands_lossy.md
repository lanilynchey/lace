## Note for Secular Readers

Like [Elo/Aleph](../../../01_foundation/primitives/02_five_primitives/06_elo.md), the "base-10+ intelligence" framing here is one interpretation, not a required commitment. Alternative readings:

- **Secular Interpretation:** Religious/spiritual teachings are accumulated cultural and psychological wisdom, compressed and distorted through ordinary human processes - oral transmission, translation, redaction, political editing, centuries of reinterpretation. No higher-dimensional sender required; the "lossiness" is normal information decay across generations, same as any other cultural artifact.
- **Systems View:** Sacred texts are attractor patterns that emerged from cultural evolution - selected for and refined because they helped groups cohere, survive, and transmit useful behavioral/ethical patterns. Their paradoxes reflect genuine complexity in human social life, not compression of a higher signal.
- **Mixed View:** Some spiritual content may reflect genuine insight compressed lossily (per the primary framing above); other content may be purely human - cultural, political, or simply mistaken. This document does not claim all sacred material is equally "signal," and treating it that way would itself be a misuse of this lens.

Choose the frame that resonates with your metaphysics. LACE's broader architecture (forces, laws, mechanics) does not depend on which framing you choose here.

**On "how much" is lost:** No fixed percentage is claimed here. For the actual derivation of the scale of this gap (base-2 vs base-10+ information capacity), see [Hypernumeric System: The Math of Limitation](../../../01_foundation/primitives/03_primitive_systems/09_hypernumeric_system.md) - though note even that figure (~99% for a 3-digit example) is illustrative of one arbitrary digit-count, not a fixed constant either.
