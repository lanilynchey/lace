## Tier 1 Activation Patterns

**Key Principle:** Tier 1 forces are **always present** - they are the axioms of reality and never fully deactivate.

### Activation Characteristics

**Typical Range:** 0.20 - 0.80
- **Rarely below 0.20:** Even in unconscious states (coma, sleep), Tier 1 forces persist
  - entropy() continues (cells decay, time passes)
  - consciousness() may be minimal but not zero (brain activity persists)
  - pattern() structures remain (body maintains form)
- **Rarely at extremes:** Tier 1 forces don't spike to 1.0 or drop to 0.0 often
  - They're foundational background constants, not volatile spikes
  - Example: entropy doesn't suddenly become 1.0 (instant decay) - it's steady

**Never Zero:**
- Tier 1 forces cannot be 0.0 while in embodied form
- Only at death threshold does consciousness() approach true 0.0
- entropy() = 0.0 would mean no change possible (violation of 2nd law of thermodynamics)

**Stability:**
- Tier 1 activations change SLOWLY (days to months, not minutes to hours)
- Building coherence from 0.40 → 0.60 takes weeks of consistent practice
- Sudden drops possible (trauma), but recovery also requires time

**Volatility (Rate of Change):**
- **Slow changes:** Days to months for significant shifts
- **Example:** Building coherence from 0.40 → 0.60 takes 4-8 weeks of daily practice
- **Example:** Entropy increase during illness: gradual over days/weeks, not instant
- **Recovery:** After trauma-induced coherence drop, recovery also requires weeks-months

**Why This Matters:**
- Agents always have some baseline of each Tier 1 force active
- Interventions targeting Tier 1 require sustained effort (not quick fixes)
- Tier 1 stability provides foundation for higher-tier volatility
- Changes compound over time - small consistent shifts create major transformation
