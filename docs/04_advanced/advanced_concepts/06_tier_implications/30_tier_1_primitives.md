# Tier 1 + Primitives: Working WITH Substrate

**Tier 1 forces build FROM primitives** (see [Primitives](../../../01_foundation/primitives/00_index.md), [Tier 1 Forces](../../../02_forces/tier_1_forces/00_index.md) for full inheritance chains).

**Primitive Relationships:**

```python
# Tier 1 forces built from primitives:
entropy() ← Δ (Delta - change/impermanence)
consciousness() ← χ (Chi - observation/awareness)
pattern() ← Φ (Phi - structure/form)
coherence() ← Φ + alignment_function
polarity() ← binary_logic + duality_emergence
```

**What You CAN Do:**
- **Work WITH Δ (Delta)** - Accept impermanence, ride change, embrace cycles
  - Practice: Impermanence meditation, letting go rituals
- **Work WITH Τ (Tau)** - Align with time's pulse (though time() is Tier 2, Τ underlies it)
  - Practice: Present moment awareness, breathwork
- **Work WITH χ (Chi)** - Sharpen observation, increase awareness recursively
  - Practice: Mindfulness, self-observation, "I am aware that I am aware"
- **Work WITH Φ (Phi)** - Recognize patterns, learn structures
  - Practice: Pattern tracking, fractal recognition
- **Work WITH א (Elo)** - Align with encoded purpose (attractor states, not fate)
  - Practice: Purpose exploration, mission alignment

**Access Level:** **Read-only** for agents
- You can OBSERVE primitives (see their effects)
- You cannot EDIT primitives (they're substrate-level)
- Primitives are constants in the system

**Key Insight:** Mastery at Tier 1 means **understanding that you're working within constraints**, not against them. The primitives define reality's substrate - your job is to work skillfully within those rules.
