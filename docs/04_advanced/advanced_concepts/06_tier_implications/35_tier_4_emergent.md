## Tier 4 Activation Patterns

**Key Principle:** Tier 4 forces are **situational and often dormant** - they activate when specific conditions align, then may disappear for long periods.

### Activation Characteristics

**Typical Range:** 0.00 - 0.80 (rarely exceeds 0.80)
- **Often zero:** Tier 4 forces can be completely absent for days, weeks, months
  - madness() = 0.0 for most people most of the time
  - courage() = 0.0 when no fear to overcome
  - war() = 0.0 during peacetime
  - art() = 0.0 when not actively creating
- **Rarely maximum:** Tier 4 forces are unstable at extreme activation
  - madness() → 1.0 = psychotic break (system collapse)
  - war() → 1.0 = total war (rare, catastrophic)
  - Most T4 forces peak around 0.60-0.80 then destabilize

**Sudden Activation:**
- Tier 4 forces can go from 0.0 → 0.7+ rapidly when conditions align
  - Seeing profound beauty → beauty() spikes from 0.0 → 0.75
  - Entering conflict → war() activates from 0.0 → 0.60
  - Creative inspiration → art() surges from 0.0 → 0.70

**Brief Peaks:**
- Tier 4 activations often UNSUSTAINABLE (brief peaks more common than sustained high)
  - Courage spike during crisis, then drops when crisis passes
  - Beauty perception intense then fades
  - Madness episodes acute then resolve (or collapse into T1 dysfunction)

**Context-Dependent:**
- Tier 4 forces require specific triggers (parent force combinations)
  - courage() needs fear + hope + will simultaneously
  - beauty() needs pattern + coherence + consciousness
  - luck() needs coherence + consciousness + action + synchronicity

**Volatility (Rate of Change):**
- **Sudden onset:** 0 → high activation in seconds to minutes when triggered
- **Example:** Beauty activation: 0.0 → 0.75 instantly upon seeing sunset
- **Example:** Courage spike: 0.0 → 0.65 in seconds when crisis demands action
- **Example:** Art activation: 0.0 → 0.70 when inspiration strikes
- **Brief duration:** Often peaks for minutes to hours, then drops back to dormant
- **Sustainability challenge:** Maintaining T4 activation requires sustaining all parent forces

**Why This Matters:**
- Tier 4 forces are emergent phenomena, not baseline states
- Agents can live entire days/weeks with multiple T4 forces at 0.0 (normal)
- When T4 activates, it's significant and often transformative
- Sustainability challenge: how to maintain T4 activation (requires maintaining parent forces)
- Brief windows of opportunity - must act when T4 force activates
