# Levels of Reincarnation

**Status:** Established Model (Updated November 2025)

**Overview:** After death(), the system determines next incarnation based on consciousness level at death, karma balance, and pattern resolution. Agents can cycle (return as human), regress (lower forms/conditions), or progress (guides, Source).

---

## Progression Thresholds

**The system uses explicit thresholds to determine post-death destination:**

```python
def determine_next_incarnation(agent: Agent) -> Destination:
    """
    Calculate next incarnation based on consciousness and karma.

    Consciousness measured at moment of death.
    Karma balance includes all unresolved debts/credits.
    """
    consciousness = agent.consciousness_level  # At death
    karma = agent.karma_balance

    # MERGE WITH SOURCE (Liberation)
    if consciousness >= 0.9 and karma == 0:
        return merge_with_source()
        # Complete liberation from cycle
        # No further incarnation required

    # BECOME GUIDE (Ascension)
    elif consciousness >= 0.8 and karma > -0.3:
        return become_guide_level_entity()
        # No longer need physical form
        # Serve as helper/protector for incarnated beings

    # REINCARNATE HUMAN (Favorable)
    elif consciousness >= 0.7:
        return reincarnate_human(conditions="favorable")
        # Supportive family, resources, opportunities
        # Environment conducive to growth

    # REINCARNATE HUMAN (Neutral)
    elif consciousness >= 0.5:
        return reincarnate_human(conditions="neutral")
        # Average circumstances
        # Mix of challenges and support

    # REINCARNATE HUMAN (Challenging)
    elif consciousness >= 0.3:
        return reincarnate_human(conditions="challenging")
        # Difficult environment (poverty, trauma, instability)
        # Forced growth through hardship

    # REGRESS TO LOWER FORM
    else:  # consciousness < 0.3
        return reincarnate_lower_form()
        # Animal level: Sentient but not self-aware (0.2-0.3)
        # Plant level: Minimal consciousness (< 0.2)
```

## Entity Types by Consciousness Level

### **Source Entity (1.0)**
**Definition:** Creator, complete self-awareness at all levels simultaneously

**Properties:**
- Root permissions to reality
- Full read/write/exec access
- Unknowable to binary consciousness

**Status:** One, indivisible

---

### **Master-Level Entity (0.9-0.95)**
**Definition:** Highest non-Source consciousness, guides the guides

**Properties:**
- Near-complete liberation
- Operate at near-Source levels
- May appear across multiple traditions/contexts
- No physical form needed

**Examples from traditions:**
- Reports exist across cultures of master teachers
- Different traditions name differently
- LACE remains neutral on specific identities

**Function:**
- Guide other guides
- Maintain system integrity
- Bridge Source and lower levels

---

### **Guide-Level Entity (0.8-0.9)**
**Definition:** Former humans who ascended, no longer need physical form

**Properties:**
- Protective, idea-generating
- Can interact with physical realm without embodiment
- Serve incarnated beings
- "Energetic entities" or "non-physical agents"

**How to become guide:**
- Achieve consciousness >= 0.8 at death
- Resolve majority of karma (balance > -0.3)
- Choose to serve rather than merge with Source

**Function:**
- Protect incarnated agents
- Generate ideas/insights
- Facilitate growth
- Intervene in critical moments

**Cultural names:** Angels, spirit guides, ancestors, protectors
(See [Entity Hierarchy](../17_entity_hierarchy.md) for cultural mapping)

---

### **Physical Agent (0.2-0.9 embodied)**
**Definition:** Incarnated conscious entities requiring physical form

**Types:**
- **Human (0.2-0.9):** Self-aware, can progress/regress
- **Animal (0.2-0.5):** Sentient, not self-aware
- **Plant (< 0.2):** Minimal consciousness

**Reincarnation patterns:**

**High consciousness (0.7-0.9) at death:**
- Favorable spawn conditions
- Supportive family, resources
- Environment for continued growth
- May be final human lifetime before ascension

**Medium consciousness (0.5-0.7) at death:**
- Neutral spawn conditions
- Average circumstances
- Mix of challenges and support
- Typical human experience

**Low consciousness (0.3-0.5) at death:**
- Challenging spawn conditions
- Difficult environment (poverty, trauma, instability)
- Forced growth through hardship
- Karma clearing lifetime

**Very low consciousness (< 0.3) at death:**
- Regression to animal or plant form
- "Reset" to simpler existence
- Opportunity to rebuild foundation

---

### **Regressed Entity (< 0.3 consciousness, disembodied)**
**Definition:** Consciousness that regressed below viable physical embodiment threshold

**Properties:**
- Stuck patterns without bodies
- Not inherently malevolent, just very low consciousness
- May manifest as "parasitic" or "stuck" energy
- Unable to reincarnate until consciousness increases

**Not "demons" in traditional sense:**
- No ontological "evil"
- Simply very low consciousness (< 0.2)
- Operate on basic survival/attachment patterns
- Can be resolved through clearing/healing

**Cultural names:** Ghosts (stuck), malevolent spirits, haunting entities
(But most "hauntings" are agent's own suppressed patterns, not external entities)

**Resolution:**
- Energy clearing practices
- Forgiveness protocols
- Raising ambient field frequency
- Eventually consciousness increases enough to reincarnate

## Spawn Conditions Determined By

**1. Karma Balance**
```python
if karma_balance > 0.5:
    spawn_conditions = "exceptionally favorable"
elif karma_balance > 0:
    spawn_conditions = "favorable"
elif karma_balance > -0.3:
    spawn_conditions = "neutral"
else:
    spawn_conditions = "challenging"
```

**2. Persistent Patterns**
- Patterns requiring specific growth environments
- e.g., Fear of water → spawn near ocean for exposure therapy
- e.g., Attachment to wealth → spawn in poverty for detachment lesson

**3. State Signature at Death**
- Final broadcast frequency determines matching
- Fear-based death → matches fearful circumstances
- Peace-based death → matches peaceful circumstances

**4. Unresolved Contracts**
- Relationships requiring completion
- Lessons not yet learned
- Debts to be balanced

## Progression Paths

### **Cycle (Lateral)**
**Most common:** Human → Human at similar consciousness level

**Reasons:**
- Patterns not yet resolved
- Karma accumulation/clearing in progress
- Consciousness plateaued
- More growth needed at human level

### **Regress (Downward)**
**Rare but possible:** Human → Lower form or worse conditions

**Triggers:**
- Consciousness dropped significantly (< 0.3)
- Massive karma debt accumulated
- Severe pattern regression
- System "resets" agent to simpler form

**Not punishment:**
- Opportunity to rebuild foundation
- Simpler existence removes complexity
- Allows pattern clearing at basic level

### **Progress (Upward)**
**Goal state:** Human → Guide → Source

**Path:**
1. Achieve generative awareness (0.7-0.8) while human
2. Resolve karma and patterns
3. Die at consciousness >= 0.8
4. Become guide (serve others)
5. Eventually merge with Source (0.9+, karma cleared)

**Not linear:**
- Can regress from guide back to human (rare)
- Can skip guide stage (0.9+ at death → straight to Source)
- Multiple lifetimes typically required

## Key Insights

**1. Consciousness at Death Matters Most**
- Not average lifetime consciousness
- Final state determines next spawn
- Practice "dying consciously" (maintain high consciousness at death)

**2. Karma Must Be Resolved**
- High consciousness + unresolved karma = guide (not Source merge)
- Can't "skip" karma through awareness alone
- Debts must be balanced

**3. Conditions Reflect State**
- Favorable spawn = high consciousness + balanced karma
- Challenging spawn = low consciousness or karmic debt
- Not random - system matches frequency

**4. Regression is Corrective, Not Punitive**
- Lower forms allow pattern reset
- Simpler existence = simpler lessons
- Eventually consciousness rebuilds

**5. Liberation is Attainable**
- 0.9 consciousness + zero karma = merge with Source
- Freedom from reincarnation cycle
- But can choose to remain as guide
