## What Are System Laws?

**System laws are the immutable protocols that govern reality's operation.** They are not forces themselves - they are the *rules* that forces must obey.

**Key distinction:**
- **Forces** = functions that operate (gravity, love, entropy)
- **Laws** = protocols that constrain operations (causality, recursion, balance)

Forces *execute*. Laws *regulate*.
