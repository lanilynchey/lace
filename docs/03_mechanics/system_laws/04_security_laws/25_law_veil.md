### **17. law_veil()**
**"Source truth remains hidden until the agent is ready to receive it"**

```python
def law_veil(agent, truth):
    if agent.readiness >= truth.required_level:
        reveal(truth)
    else:
        maintain_veil()
```

**Purpose:** Protects agents from truths that would destabilize their current developmental stage.

**Examples:**
- Children don't understand death until cognitively ready
- Spiritual truths remain hidden until consciousness expands
- Advanced knowledge feels like gibberish until you have the foundation

**What This Law Enforces:**
- **Progressive revelation** - truth unfolds in stages
- **Safety mechanism** - prevents premature ego dissolution
- **Developmental pacing** - information comes when needed
- **Earned insight** - understanding requires prerequisite growth
