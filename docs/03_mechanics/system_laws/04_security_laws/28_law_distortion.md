### **20. law_distortion()**
**"Core truth obfuscates itself to prevent misuse by unaligned agents"**

```python
def law_distortion(truth, receiver):
    if receiver.alignment < threshold:
        return encode(truth, symbolic=True, indirect=True)
    else:
        return truth  # Direct transmission
```

**Purpose:** Sacred knowledge self-encrypts when transmitted to unprepared receivers.

**Examples:**
- Religious texts become literal/dogmatic when misunderstood
- Mystical teachings sound like nonsense to the uninitiated
- Advanced concepts appear as paradoxes or koans
- Truth "dumbs itself down" for safety

**What This Law Enforces:**
- **Self-protection of truth** - wisdom guards itself
- **Symbolic encoding** - high ideas compress into parables
- **Prevents weaponization** - dangerous knowledge stays hidden
- **Requires decoding work** - understanding must be earned

### **Critical Distinctions**

**1. DESCRIPTIVE vs PRESCRIPTIVE:**

This law describes HOW reality actually operates, not how it SHOULD operate.
- Sacred knowledge DOES self-encrypt based on receiver capacity
- This is a safety mechanism, not a value judgment

**2. RECEIVER READINESS vs ELITISM:**

**Healthy:** "Deep truths require preparation to integrate safely"
- Example: Mystical ego-dissolution experiences need grounded psyche
- Example: Advanced physics requires foundational mathematics
- Protective mechanism preventing psychological harm

**Corrupted:** "Some people deserve truth, others don't"
- Example: Gatekeeping using "you're not ready" to maintain power
- Example: Deliberately obscuring truth to control others
- Elitist justification for withholding knowledge

**3. TRANSMISSION QUALITY ALSO MATTERS:**

law_distortion() operates on RECEIVER CAPACITY.

**BUT:** Poor transmission is also real:
- Teachers can obscure truth through incompetence, not just protection
- Institutions can corrupt teachings through dogma
- Religious texts can be deliberately distorted by power structures

**Both/and:**
- Truth DOES self-encrypt for safety (law_distortion operates)
- AND humans can misuse this as justification for gatekeeping
- AND some teachings are just poorly transmitted

**Warning:** Do not use law_distortion() to blame seekers for not understanding. Always examine: Is the teaching itself clear? Is the receiver ready? Both matter.
