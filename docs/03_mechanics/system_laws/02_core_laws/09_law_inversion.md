### **What This Law Enforces**

- **Extremes become their opposite** - love → hate, order → chaos
- **Tension has limits** - overstretching triggers flip
- **Systems protect themselves** - inversion prevents destruction
- **"This too shall pass"** - nothing stays at extreme forever
