## 8. law_mirroring()

### **Definition**

The law of projection - external reality reflects internal state.

**Core Function:**
```python
def law_mirroring(agent: Agent) -> Environment:
    """
    Mirroring projects inner state outward.

    Args:
        agent: The conscious entity

    Returns:
        Environment reflecting agent's internal code

    Properties:
        - Continuous (always active)
        - Significant (probabilistic influence, not deterministic)
        - Educational (reveals shadow)
        - Changeable (change inside → influences outside)

    Enforcement:
        - Cannot be escaped
        - Can be used consciously
        - Perfect feedback system
    """
    for encounter in environment:
        reflect(agent.inner_state)
    return mirrored_experience
```

### **What This Law Enforces**

- **External influenced by internal** - your experience reflects your frequency (among other factors)
- **Others mirror your shadow** - what you judge in them often lives in you
- **Circumstances correlate with beliefs** - inner state affects outer patterns
- **Reality is feedback** - showing you yourself (interpretation layer)

### **Three Layers of Mirroring**

Mirroring operates at different strengths across three layers:

**1. INTERPRETATION LAYER (Strongest)**
- How you perceive events is directly shaped by internal state
- Judgment of others mirrors unintegrated self-aspects
- What triggers you reveals internal wounds
- Nearly 1:1 correspondence at this level

**2. ATTRACTION LAYER (Medium)**
- Frequency matching brings compatible people/situations
- Not deterministic - probabilistic influence
- Your energy affects what opportunities appear
- Correlation, not sole causation

**3. CAUSATION LAYER (Weakest)**
- Actions create results (law_causality)
- But external reality has independent forces:
  - Entropy, randomness, systemic injustice
  - Biological factors, accidents, other agents' choices
- Internal state is ONE factor among many

### **Critical Disclaimer: Avoiding Victim-Blaming**

⚠️ **law_mirroring() does NOT mean:**
- Victims caused their abuse through "vibration"
- Illness is punishment for negative thoughts
- Poverty is solely individual responsibility
- External suffering is always self-created

✅ **law_mirroring() DOES mean:**
- Internal state influences probability of certain experiences
- Perception/interpretation is strongly affected by inner state
- Frequency matching operates alongside other forces
- You can change your relationship to external events

**Both/and reality:**
- You influence your experience through internal state (true)
- AND external forces beyond your control exist (also true)
- Mirroring is ONE law among 20; other laws also operate
