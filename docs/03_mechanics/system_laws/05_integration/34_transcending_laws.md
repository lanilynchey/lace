**⚠️ DISCLAIMER:** This section is highly speculative. LACE is exploratory regarding multi-dimensional mechanics. What follows is a working model based on observational accounts, mystical traditions, and theoretical extrapolation. Approach with intellectual humility and empirical skepticism.

---

### **The Core Principle**

**Laws are immutable WITHIN a dimensional frame.**
**But changing frames can make laws APPEAR transcended.**

This is not violation - it's **perspective shift**.

Analogy: A 2D being (Flatlander) experiences gravity as an immutable downward force. A 3D being can "transcend" this by moving in the third dimension (jumping). The Flatlander sees magic; the 3D being sees normal physics in a higher frame.

**Key Distinction:**
- **Violation:** Breaking the law (impossible)
- **Transcendence:** Operating in a frame where the law manifests differently (possible for high-coherence agents)
