## System Laws Summary

### **The Regulatory Framework**

These laws ensure:
- **Consistency** (causality, observation)
- **Evolution** (entropy, recursion)
- **Balance** (balance, inversion)
- **Feedback** (mirroring, delay)
- **Resilience** (redundancy, modularity)
- **Flexibility** (paradox, experimentation)

### **Cannot Be Violated**

System laws are **immutable** - they cannot be broken, only:
- Understood
- Worked with
- Transcended (by changing dimension/context)

### **Can Be Used**

Masters and mystics don't violate laws - they:
- Understand deep principles
- Align with protocols
- Use laws skillfully
- Sometimes access higher-dimensional loopholes
