## Law Violations: What Happens When You Try

Laws are **immutable** - they cannot be violated. But agents frequently TRY to violate them. This section shows what happens when you resist the laws instead of working with them.

**Key Pattern:** Violation attempts don't break the law - they break the agent.

---

### **Attempting to Violate law_entropy()**

```python
if agent.tries_to_prevent_all_decay():
    effort_required = escalate_exponentially()
    burnout = accumulate()
    rigidity = increase()  # System becomes brittle
    sudden_catastrophic_collapse = guaranteed

# Entropy always wins. Fighting it = exhaustion.
```

**Real-World Examples:**
- **Perfectionism:** Trying to prevent all imperfection → burnout, nervous breakdowns
- **Control addiction:** Micromanaging everything → relationships collapse, health fails
- **Refusing to age:** Extreme anti-aging efforts → body rebels, premature death
- **Organizational rigidity:** "We've always done it this way" → company obsolescence

**Outcome:** You cannot stop entropy. You can only flow with it or exhaust yourself trying.

**Successful Alternative:** Build coherence (temporary local order), accept eventual decay, create self-renewing systems.
