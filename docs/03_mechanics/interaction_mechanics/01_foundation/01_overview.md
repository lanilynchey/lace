## Overview

**Forces don't operate in isolation.** Reality is the result of countless simultaneous interactions - forces colliding, amplifying, canceling, and synthesizing into new patterns.

This document maps the **interaction layer** - the dynamic engine that generates complexity from simplicity.

Think of it as:
- **Chemistry** (how elements combine into molecules)
- **Ecology** (how species interact in ecosystems)
- **Music** (how notes interact to create harmony/dissonance)
- **Code** (how functions call and modify each other)

**This is where 26 forces become infinite possibilities.**
