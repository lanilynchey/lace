## Overview

**Every force in LACE has a lineage.** This document traces the complete inheritance chains - from primordial axioms down through all descendants.

Think of this as:
- **Genealogical tree** (who begat whom)
- **Dependency graph** (what requires what)
- **Class hierarchy** (OOP inheritance structure)
- **Flow diagram** (from source to expression)

**This makes LACE's inheritance structure fully traceable** - you can trace any force back to its axioms and forward to its descendants. This dependency completeness is the foundation for computational implementation (see FUNCTION_LIBRARY.md for executable specifications).

**Note on Primitives:** All forces ultimately derive from the five primitives (Δ, Τ, χ, Φ, א) documented in PRIMITIVES.md. This document traces inheritance from Tier 1 axioms downward; for the substrate layer below axioms, see PRIMITIVES.md.
