# Implications & Predictions

## **If LACE is Correct, We Should Observe:**

1. **Hierarchical Structure in Everything**
   - Simple primitives combining into complexity
   - Recursion and self-similarity across scales (fractals)
   - **Observation:** Yes - atoms → molecules → cells → organisms → ecosystems

2. **Deterministic-ish Behavior at Macro Scale**
   - Probabilistic at quantum level, but patterns emerge
   - **Observation:** Yes - statistical mechanics, thermodynamics

3. **Feedback Loops Creating Stability and Instability**
   - Systems self-regulate or spiral
   - **Observation:** Yes - homeostasis, phase transitions, tipping points

4. **Information Cannot Be Destroyed**
   - Only transformed (like conservation of energy)
   - **Observation:** Unclear - physics suggests yes (holographic principle), but unproven

5. **Consciousness Correlates with Recursive Complexity**
   - More self-referential systems = more "aware"
   - **Observation:** Unclear - hard to measure, but suggestive

6. **Paradoxes Arise at Perception Boundaries**
   - Wave-particle duality, free will vs. determinism, etc.
   - **Observation:** Yes - quantum mechanics, philosophy full of these

7. **Mystical States Reveal Non-Binary Perception**
   - Non-dual awareness, ego death, unity experiences
   - **Observation:** Consistently reported across cultures
