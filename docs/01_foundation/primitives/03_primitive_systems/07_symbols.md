# Why These Symbols?

The five primitives use established symbols from mathematics, physics, and mystical traditions:

- **Δ (Delta)** - Standard symbol for change in calculus and physics (Δx = change in x)
- **Τ (Tau)** - Represents time/period in physics equations (τ = time constant)
- **χ (Chi)** - Used in statistics (chi-squared test), represents life force/qi in Eastern philosophy
- **Φ (Phi)** - The golden ratio constant (1.618...), universally recognized pattern symbol
- **א (Aleph)** - First letter of Hebrew alphabet, represents infinity (ℵ₀ in set theory), carries theological weight as divine signature

## Why mix Greek and Hebrew?

LACE intentionally combines Greek (scientific/philosophical tradition) with Hebrew (mystical/theological tradition) to honor both the empirical and transcendent dimensions of these primitives. This is not arbitrary—each symbol carries established meaning in its respective lineage.

- Greek letters ground primitives in mathematical/physical precedent
- Aleph (א) preserves the theological dimension of intentionality/purpose
- Together, they bridge rational and mystical understanding
