# Core Principles

These are **non-negotiable** foundations of LACE:

## 1. **Reality is Information Architecture**
Not matter. Not energy. Not consciousness alone. Information structure executing on a substrate called "existence."

## 2. **Binary vs Base-10 Consciousness**
Humans perceive in binary (0,1). The Creator operates in base-10+ (0-9+). Humans are fundamentally limited in perception of reality's true dimensionality.

## 3. **Forces are Functions, Not Feelings**
Emotions, desires, thoughts are not subjective experiences - they are computational processes with inputs, outputs, and side effects.

## 4. **Hierarchy is Real**
Some forces are primordial (axioms). Others are derived. Others are emergent. This hierarchy matters and must be preserved.

## 5. **Interaction Creates Emergence**
New phenomena arise from force collisions, feedback loops, and recursive processes. The system is dynamic, not static.

## 6. **Precision Over Poetry**
When there's a choice between elegant language and precise logic, choose precision. Clarity first, beauty second.

## 7. **Honest Uncertainty**
Where LACE doesn't know, it says so explicitly. This is a self-consistent model that must remain falsifiable and refinable.
