# Root Tracking

## **Root Tracking**

**Definition:** Tracing recurring patterns back through force layers to source force/primitive to identify root cause and intervention point

**Methodology (Conceptual):**

**5-Layer Reverse Engineering:**
```
Step 1: Identify surface pattern (life issue/recurring theme)
    ↓
Step 2: Trace to Tier 4 force (emergent pattern)
    ↓
Step 3: Trace to Tier 3 force (interface/emotional driver)
    ↓
Step 4: Trace to Tier 2 force (governing mechanism)
    ↓
Step 5: Trace to Tier 1 force (primordial foundation)
    ↓
Optional Step 6: Trace to primitive (substrate level)
```

**Practical Example:**

**Surface Pattern:** "I keep attracting unavailable romantic partners"

**Step 1 - Identify Pattern:**
- Observable: 5 relationships in row, all with emotionally unavailable people
- Consequence: Loneliness, frustration, feeling unloveable

**Step 2 - Trace to Tier 4 (Emergent):**
- Could involve: beauty() (attracted to surface appeal), art() (romanticizing unavailability)
- Analysis: Drawn to "potential" and "mystery" (art/madness intersection)

**Step 3 - Trace to Tier 3 (Interface/Emotional):**
- **Primary force:** fear() - "If they get too close, they'll see I'm unworthy"
- Secondary: desire() - "I want love but fear intimacy"
- Result: desire() + fear() = approach-avoidance pattern
- Subconscious solution: Choose people who CAN'T get close (unavailable)

**Step 4 - Trace to Tier 2 (Governing):**
- memory() - Childhood wound: "When I was vulnerable, I got hurt"
- Pattern stored as: "vulnerability = danger"
- truth() encoding: "I am unworthy of secure love" (false but embodied)

**Step 5 - Trace to Tier 1 (Primordial):**
- entropy() - Pattern seeking dissolution (relationships decay)
- polarity() - Push-pull dynamic (want/fear creates charge)
- **ROOT:** coherence() LACK - internal misalignment between desire (want love) and belief (unworthy)

**Step 6 - Trace to Primitive:**
- Φ (Phi/Pattern) - Symmetry: "I was rejected → I reject (by choosing unavailable)"
- Δ (Delta/Change) - Resistance: Fear of change (vulnerability = change from defended state)

**Intervention Point Identified:**
- **Surface intervention:** "Just choose better partners" → Won't work (doesn't address root)
- **Tier 3 intervention:** Work with fear() and desire() forces (therapy, emotional processing)
- **Tier 2 intervention:** Rewrite memory encoding and truth beliefs (deeper reprogramming)
- **Tier 1 intervention:** Build coherence (align belief + expectation + embodiment)
- **Primitive intervention:** Release Δ resistance to vulnerability change

**Most Effective:** Multi-layer approach, but Tier 1 (coherence building) and Tier 2 (memory/truth rewriting) are leverage points

**Key Insight:** Root tracking prevents surface-level "solutions" that don't address actual cause
