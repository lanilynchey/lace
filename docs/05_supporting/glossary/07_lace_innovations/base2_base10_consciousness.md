**Already defined above** - See "Base-2 (Binary)" and "Base-10+ (Multi-State)" in Philosophical Terms section

**Why This Is Novel:**
Most metaphysical frameworks discuss "higher consciousness" or "expanded awareness" without specifying the mechanism. LACE provides computational model: human limitation is dimensional (base-2 vs base-10+), not just degree of sophistication.

**Implications:**
- Paradox is structural (base-2 encountering base-10+ phenomena)
- Mystical experiences = brief glimpses of higher-dimensional states
- Enlightenment = recognizing the base-2 constraint
- Non-duality teachings = describing base-10+ from base-2 language
- "God works in mysterious ways" = base-10+ operations we cannot perceive
