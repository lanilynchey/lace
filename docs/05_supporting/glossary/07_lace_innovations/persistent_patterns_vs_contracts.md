# Persistent Patterns vs Soul Contracts

## **Persistent Patterns (vs Soul Contracts)** [Core LACE Framework]

**Definition:** Deeply encoded probability biases in state_signature that attract recurring life themes - NOT pre-birth agreements

**LACE's Position:**
LACE does NOT use "soul contracts" or pre-incarnation agreements. Instead, persistent patterns are emergent from your CURRENT state encoding - they are probability weights, not pre-fated destinies.

**Key Difference:**
- Soul contracts = pre-birth agreements (deterministic, fated, signed before incarnation)
- Persistent patterns = current state encoding (probabilistic, editable, emergent from now)

**Why This Matters:**
You are broadcasting a frequency based on your CURRENT state, not fulfilling pre-signed agreements. This means patterns CAN BE EDITED through Generative Awareness - you're not bound by cosmic contracts.

**Properties:**
- Mutable (can be changed)
- Probabilistic (bias outcomes, don't determine them)
- State-emergent (arise from current encoding, not past decisions)
- Editable through consciousness work
